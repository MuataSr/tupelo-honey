Built from the ground up by a veteran educator.

Tupelo wasn’t created by a massive corporate test-prep machine. It is an Open Educational Resource engineered by an educator with over 25 years of collegiate teaching experience.

After decades of managing a college learning support center, the pattern became clear: brilliant, capable nursing students were hitting a wall. They weren’t failing because they lacked compassion or intelligence—they were failing because traditional test prep was expensive, inaccessible, and taught rote memorization instead of clinical reasoning.

Tupelo changes that. It brings decades of instructional design and tutoring center expertise directly to your screen, for free, so you can walk into your exam with total confidence.
