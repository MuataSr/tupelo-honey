Stimulus Training

The TEAS/HESI uses stimulus passages — excerpts from historical documents, court rulings, and speeches. Learning to read them efficiently is the single most valuable skill you can develop.

Why stimulus literacy matters

  • ✦ ~{{ total_stimulus }} of {{ "{:,}".format(total_questions) }} TEAS/HESI questions use stimulus passages
  • ✦ Most students lose points not from lack of knowledge, but from misreading the passage
  • ✦ This training teaches you a 3-step method used by top scorers

The 3-Step Method

1
Read & Identify

Read the passage. Identify the document type, author, era, and main argument.

2
Annotate

Jot down key phrases, claims, and anything that connects to TEAS/HESI concepts you've studied.

3
Predict & Answer

Read the question. Before looking at options, predict what the answer should be. Then select.
